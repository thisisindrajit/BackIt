# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Builders:** indie hackers and small SaaS teams with early traction (about USD 1K+ monthly revenue for three consecutive months). They need distribution and non-dilutive upfront cash, and they set up a campaign in one sitting at a desk.
- **Backers:** friends, peers, and fellow indie hackers who believe in a builder. They pre-buy at a founding price, share a personal referral link, and check their earnings occasionally, often on a phone.
- **Referred buyers:** people who arrive through a backer's link, usually on mobile, and decide whether to buy at a discount.

## Product Purpose

BackIt (backit.club) lets builders run founding-backer campaigns. Backers pre-buy the product through PayPal checkout, receive a permanent founding badge and referral link, and earn commissions on referred sales. BackIt notifies the builder's app by signed webhook so product access is real. An AI agent drafts campaign copy and referral posts and prepares payout previews; a human approves every payout. Success for the hackathon MVP is one complete, verifiable sandbox loop: publish, founding purchase, referral sale, product access, refund handling, and an approved payout.

## Positioning

Backers earn only from purchases and referrals; there is no equity, revenue share, or investment return. Every number shown comes from captured payments and an append-only ledger, and every payout is a human-approved, exact preview. Mock or sandbox evidence is always labelled.

## Operating Context

- Hackathon sandbox only: PayPal sandbox (or labelled mock mode), USD, one-time payments.
- Money appears as exact amounts; financial states are communicated in text, not color alone.
- Public pages show pseudonymous ledger entries and never emails, payer IDs, or payout destinations.

## Brand Commitments

- Typefaces: Timeless Serif (primary: headlines, money, stories) and Timeless Sans (secondary: interface and body), local copies of the installed Timeless family. CTA buttons use white text on sage.
- Colors: primary `#83a281`, secondary `#c7d3c0`, accent `#fff9d6`.
- Interface: minimal and simple, built only with shadcn/ui and Tailwind CSS.

## Accessibility

WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, keyboard navigation with visible focus, reduced-motion support, and mobile-friendly checkout and tables.
